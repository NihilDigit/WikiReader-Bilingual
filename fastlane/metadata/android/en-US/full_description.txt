<p>WikiReader Bilingual is a lightweight Android app for reading Wikipedia articles with the original text and an AI-assisted translation side by side. It keeps WikiReader's distraction-free reading experience while adding bilingual article content for language learning.</p>

<p>Features</p>
<ul>
    <li><b>Fast loading:</b> The article text is loaded before anything else, so you can get to reading, quick</li>
    <li><b>Bilingual reading:</b> Read article paragraphs with translated text under the original</li>
    <li><b>Configurable translation:</b> Use a DeepSeek API key, choose the target Wikipedia language, and adjust translation concurrency</li>
    <li><b>Manual translation mode:</b> Show paragraph placeholders and translate only what you request</li>
    <li><b>Context help:</b> Translate a sentence or ask for a short explanation of a word in context</li>
    <li><b>Feed:</b> Read the article of the day, find trending articles and get up to speed on the news with the Wikipedia Feed</li>
    <li><b>Article image:</b> View an image of the topic from its Wikipedia page. Click on it to enlarge it and view in full-screen</li>
    <li><b>Random article:</b> Feeling lucky? Click the random article button to read a random article</li>
    <li><b>Choose your language: Choose from over 300 languages on Wikipedia</b></li>
    <li><b>Save articles:</b> Download articles to your device for offline reading</li>
    <li><b>One-handed use:</b> Use the floating action buttons at the bottom for a complete one-handed experience</li>
    <li><b>Lightweight:</b> The app starts instantly, and works smoothly</li>
    <li><b>Material 3 Expressive:</b> Designed according to the latest Material 3 Expressive design
        guidelines
    </li>
    <li><b>Smooth animations:</b> Smooth and fluent animations</li>
    <li><b>Customizable colors:</b> Choose from light/dark themes and customize the Material 3 color palette</li>
    <li><b>Customizable font size:</b> Choose your own comfortable font size</li>
    <li><b>Data saver:</b> Save your limited data plan by loading text only</li>
    <li><b>Math expressions:</b> View properly rendered mathematical expressions for easily reading mathematical articles</li>
</ul>
